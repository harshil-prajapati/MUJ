Getting here

Reaching the Dehmi Kalan campus

By road

Frequent Volvo and deluxe bus services run between Jaipur and Delhi. The Jaipur bus stand is about 25 km from campus; a taxi is the easiest last leg.

By rail

Jaipur Railway Station connects to Delhi, Agra, Mumbai and Chennai, and sits roughly 24 km from the campus.
