Seven decades of Manipal, eleven years in Jaipur

MUJ was established under a Rajasthan state legislative act in 2011, carrying forward the academic values of the wider Manipal Education and Medical Group.

Overview

Part of a wider academic family

The Manipal Education and Medical Group has been a name in Indian higher education and healthcare for more than seventy years, with institutions that have collectively taught learners from dozens of countries.

Manipal University Jaipur was established in Jaipur under an Act of the Rajasthan State Legislature (Act No. 21 of 2011), making it a state private university recognised by the University Grants Commission.

What began as a single campus off the Jaipur–Ajmer Expressway has grown into a multidisciplinary university spread across four faculties, nine schools and more than thirty departments, teaching upwards of 14,000 students.

The University sits within a larger network that includes Manipal Academy of Higher Education in Karnataka, along with sister institutions in Sikkim, Malaysia and the Caribbean — giving MUJ students access to a genuinely international academic community.

Faculty numbers have grown alongside the student body: more than 700 teaching staff now work across engineering, design, law, management, media, sciences and health disciplines.

What guides us

Vision, mission & the values in between

Vision

To be recognised as a university where teaching, research and industry practice reinforce one another — producing graduates who are equally comfortable in a laboratory, a courtroom, a newsroom or a boardroom.

Mission

To offer career-oriented, multidisciplinary education supported by modern infrastructure, an active research culture, and strong industry and international partnerships.

Our history

Milestones along the way

1953

The Manipal Education Group's journey begins

The wider Manipal Education and Medical Group takes root in Manipal, Karnataka — the legacy MUJ traces its "seventy-one years" back to.

2011

Manipal University Jaipur is established

Set up by an Act of the Rajasthan State Legislature (Act No. 21 of 2011) as a state private university, with its campus at Dehmi Kalan, off the Jaipur–Ajmer Expressway.

Growth years

Four faculties, nine schools

New schools and departments are added across engineering, architecture and design, law, and health sciences, alongside the founding faculties of science and management.

Today

20,000+ students, 800+ faculty

MUJ now runs NBA-accredited B.Tech programmes, dedicated Central Research Facilities, and a growing list of international academic partnerships.
